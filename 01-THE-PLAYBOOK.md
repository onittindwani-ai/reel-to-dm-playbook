# The Playbook: Turning Reels into DMs

This is my full system. Read it once from top to bottom. After that, you only need the checklists.

## How to use this file

- First time: read it all (about 15 minutes).
- Every Reel after that: use the "Before posting checklist" and the "First hour routine", then `07-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`.

## Why comment to DM works

There are 2 wins, and you need both.

1. **The comment helps the Reel.** A comment is a clear signal that someone cared enough to act. It also starts a small conversation under your post.
2. **The DM delivers something real.** The person gets the thing you promised, in a private chat with you. That chat is where a viewer becomes someone who knows you.

If you only chase comments, people feel tricked. If you only send links, you skip the conversation. The system works when the comment is easy and the DM is worth it.

What I do not claim: I do not know exactly how Instagram ranks Reels, and I will not tell you comments "boost reach" by some number. I cannot prove that from outside Meta, and I have not seen anyone else prove it either. Learn from your own numbers (see `07-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`).

## The 6 parts

| Part | What it is | My example (AUTO Reel) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. The offer | The 1 useful thing you give away | This playbook |
| 2. The keyword | The 1 word people comment | AUTO |
| 3. The spoken ending line | The last line of your Reel that asks for the comment | "If you want MY playbook for turning Reels into DMs, the exact way I pick the keyword, or write the DM and the reply variations, then just comment the word AUTO" |
| 4. The DM | The message the app sends | A warm hello, the link, what it does, the easiest way to use it |
| 5. The comment replies | The public reply under each comment | "Sent! Check your DMs 🙌" and more |
| 6. The follow up | What you say when they reply to the DM | A thank you, an answer, or a next step |

### Part 1: The offer

Choose an offer worth commenting for. Run it through these 5 checks:

- [ ] **Specific.** "My 7 hook templates" beats "my marketing tips".
- [ ] **Useful.** It solves 1 problem the viewer has right now.
- [ ] **Instantly usable.** They can use it today, in under 10 minutes.
- [ ] **Matches the Reel.** The Reel shows the idea. The offer helps them do it.
- [ ] **Real and ready.** It exists before you post. Never promise something you have not made.

Good offer types:

| Offer type | Example | Why it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A template | A one page content calendar | They fill it in and use it |
| A prompt pack | 10 prompts to plan a week of meals | Copy, paste, done |
| A setup guide | How I set up a free voice cloning app, with a consent checklist | Saves them the trial and error |
| A checklist | My 12 point pre flight checklist for a Reel | Easy to follow on a phone |
| A skill | My free Show, Don't Tell AI skill on GitHub | They give it to Claude and it works |
| A discount code | 15 percent off a product I really use (disclose if you earn from it) | Instant value |

Weak offers: "DM me for more info", a link to your homepage, a long ebook few people finish, anything you would be embarrassed to send.

### Part 2: The keyword

1 word, 3 to 6 letters, easy to type, names the thing. Full rules and 60+ examples are in `02-KEYWORD-PICKER.md`.

### Part 3: The spoken ending line

The ending line has 3 jobs: say what they get, say why it is useful, say the exact word.

Formula:

```
If you want (what they get), (why it helps or what is inside), then just comment the word (your keyword).
```

My real examples:

- AUTO: "If you want MY playbook for turning Reels into DMs, the exact way I pick the keyword, or write the DM and the reply variations, then just comment the word AUTO"
- SHOW: the Reel about my Show, Don't Tell AI skill ended by asking people to comment SHOW to get the skill.
- VOICE: the Reel about a free voice cloning app ended by asking people to comment VOICE to get my setup guide and consent checklist.

Tips:

- Say the keyword clearly, and show it on screen at the same moment.
- Say it once at the end. If the Reel is long, you can also tease it once early ("stay to the end, I'll give you my setup guide").
- Do not say "link in bio" in the same Reel. One action only.

### Part 4: The DM

Use the 4 part formula: warm hello plus what it is, the link, what it does for them, the easiest way to use it. My real DM and 12+ templates are in `03-DM-TEMPLATES.md`.

### Part 5: The comment replies

The app replies under each comment so people know the DM is on its way. Rotate 10 to 20 variations so it reads naturally. See `04-COMMENT-REPLY-VARIATIONS.md`.

### Part 6: The follow up

When someone replies to your DM, a real conversation has started. Answer like a person, not a bot. Templates are in `03-DM-TEMPLATES.md`.

## Before posting checklist

- [ ] The resource exists, and the link opens on a phone without a login (unless the login is the point).
- [ ] I opened the link in a private browser window to check it is public.
- [ ] The keyword passed the 5 question test.
- [ ] The keyword is the same in the spoken line, the on screen text, the caption and the pinned comment.
- [ ] The DM is under about 70 words, has 1 link, and has no hype.
- [ ] 10 to 20 comment replies are loaded in the app.
- [ ] The automation is turned on for THIS Reel (not an old one).
- [ ] I tested it from a second account: commented the keyword, got the reply and the DM.
- [ ] If the resource needs a follow (Follow Unlock), I tested that too.
- [ ] If any link earns me money, the DM says so.
- [ ] I have 1 hour free after posting.

## First hour routine

1. Post the Reel.
2. Pin your own comment: "Comment AUTO and I'll send you the playbook" (with your keyword).
3. Comment the keyword yourself from your second account. Check the reply and the DM arrive.
4. Every 10 to 15 minutes, open the comments. Check the app is replying.
5. Look for comments the app missed: typos, the keyword inside a sentence, a question. Reply to those by hand.
6. Answer real questions in the comments yourself. A human reply beats a bot reply.
7. Open your DMs and answer anyone who replied to the auto DM.
8. Write down the number of comments and DMs sent after 1 hour in your tracker.

## What to do when people reply to the DM

A reply is the best thing that can happen. It means a real conversation started.

| They say | What you do |
| --- | --- |
| "Thanks!" | Say you are welcome and ask 1 easy question ("What will you use it for?") |
| A question about the resource | Answer it in 1 to 3 short lines |
| "It didn't work" | Ask what they see, send the fix or a simpler step |
| "Do you have more?" | Send 1 more relevant thing, or tell them what you post next |
| Nothing | That is fine. Do not chase them |

Never: send 3 messages in a row, push a sale they did not ask for, or copy paste the same follow up to every person.

## Mistakes that break it

1. **The offer does not match the Reel.** A fitness Reel that gives away a marketing guide. People feel tricked.
2. **The resource is not ready.** You promise it and then make people wait.
3. **The link is broken or private.** Always test in a private window.
4. **The keyword is a common word.** "Yes" or "nice" fires the DM for people who never asked.
5. **Different words in different places.** You say AUTO, the caption says AUTOMATE, the app listens for DM.
6. **The automation is on the wrong Reel.** Check before you post.
7. **The DM is too long or too salesy.** It reads like spam and people do not open the link.
8. **You never look at the comments.** The app misses typos and questions. You do not.
9. **The same reply 300 times.** It looks like a bot. Rotate.
10. **Asking for a comment and a follow and a share and a save.** One action only.
11. **Reusing a keyword every week.** People stop noticing. Change it per Reel.
12. **Promising results.** "This will 10x your followers" is a promise you cannot keep.

## What happens behind the scenes with Meta

I learned this part because I am building my own comment to DM system. As of 18 October 2026 I am still waiting on Meta business verification, so I use Vyral for my Reels. Here is how it works under the hood, using only facts from Meta's own developer pages (links in Sources below).

### Who can do this

- Apps like this use the **Instagram API with Instagram Login**. It is for Instagram **professional accounts** (business or creator). It does not need a Facebook Page.
- The developer must build a **business type Meta app** and add the **Instagram product** to it.

So: you need a professional account (business or creator). A personal account cannot use these apps.

### What the app asks permission for

| Permission | What it lets the app do |
| --- | --- |
| `instagram_business_basic` | Read your basic profile and media |
| `instagram_business_manage_comments` | Read comments and reply to them |
| `instagram_business_manage_messages` | Send and read messages |

When you connect an app, you are giving it these powers over your account. Only connect apps you trust.

### How the app gets approved

- To serve the public, an app needs **Advanced Access**. That needs **App Review** for each permission, and **Business Verification**.
- In App Review, Meta's guide says: "Any requested permission or feature missing a screen recording will not be approved". The app also needs a privacy policy URL and a 1024x1024 app icon.
- Meta says a decision usually comes "within a week".

### The rules for the DM itself (private replies)

This is the part that matters most for creators:

- The app can send **only 1 message** to the person who commented.
- It must be sent **within 7 days** of the comment.
- If the person **follows you**, it lands in their **Inbox**. If they **do not follow you**, it lands in their **Request folder** (message requests).
- The app can send **more messages only after they reply**, and then **within 24 hours** of their reply.

What this means for you:

- Put everything in that 1 DM: the link and how to use it. You do not get a second automatic message unless they reply.
- Tell non followers to check message requests (see the replies in `04-COMMENT-REPLY-VARIATIONS.md`).
- Old Reels: comments older than 7 days cannot get an automatic DM.

### Speed limit

Meta allows **750 calls per hour per Instagram professional account** for private replies. If a Reel takes off, an app may queue DMs, so some people get theirs a bit later. Each app handles this its own way.

## Safety rules

- Only connect apps you trust. You are giving them access to your DMs and comments. Read the privacy policy.
- Never put passwords, private links or personal data in a DM template.
- Disclose affiliate links and paid partnerships in the DM.
- Do not use the DM to promise income, health results or anything you cannot back up.
- Respect people who do not reply. 1 automatic message is the rule for a reason.
- Turn the automation off when the Reel's offer ends (for example, a discount code that expired).

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Likely cause | What to try |
| --- | --- | --- |
| DM not arriving at all | Automation off, set on the wrong Reel, or the app lost its connection | Check the app shows the right Reel and is on. Reconnect your account. Test from a second account |
| DM goes to message requests | The person does not follow you (this is Meta's rule) | Add "check your message requests" to some replies. Use Follow Unlock only if it fits your offer |
| Some comments get no DM | Typos or extra text, for example "auto", "Auto", "AUTO 🔥", "AUTO pls", "autto" | Check the app's match setting (contains vs exact). Add common variants as extra keywords. Reply by hand to the rest |
| The keyword is inside a sentence and not matched | App set to exact match | Switch to "contains" if the app has it, but only if your keyword is not a common word |
| People who did not ask get the DM | Keyword is too common | Pick a rarer word next time (see `02-KEYWORD-PICKER.md`) |
| Comments missing from the post | Instagram's Hidden Words filter may hide comments it thinks are offensive or spam | Open the Reel's comments and scroll to the bottom for "View hidden comments". Also check your Hidden Words settings in Instagram's privacy settings |
| Link does not open | Private, deleted, or needs a login | Open it in a private browser window. Fix it, then update the DM in the app |
| DMs arrive late | The app is queueing because of the rate limit or a busy period | Wait. Tell people in a reply that DMs may take a few minutes |
| Old comments got nothing | Comments older than 7 days cannot get a private reply | Reply by hand in the comments, or ask them to DM you the keyword |

Exact settings names differ by app and by version. If you cannot find a setting, check the app's help page.
