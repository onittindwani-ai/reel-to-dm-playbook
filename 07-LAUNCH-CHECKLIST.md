# Launch Checklist

Tick these off for every Reel. Print it, or copy it into your notes app.

## How to use this file

Go top to bottom. Each section has a time. Write your numbers in `templates/REEL-DM-TRACKER.csv` on day 2 and day 7.

## The day before

- [ ] The resource is finished and the link opens in a private browser window on a phone.
- [ ] The keyword passed the 5 question test (`02-KEYWORD-PICKER.md`).
- [ ] The keyword is not one I used in the last 7 days.
- [ ] The spoken ending line is in my script and the keyword shows on screen at the same moment.
- [ ] The DM is written (under about 70 words, 1 link, no hype, disclosure if needed).
- [ ] 10 to 20 comment replies are picked, including 2 that mention message requests.
- [ ] The pinned comment and the 1 line caption are written.
- [ ] The automation is set up in my app with the right keyword, DM and replies.
- [ ] I tested it from a second account (follower and non follower).

## Posting time

- [ ] Post the Reel.
- [ ] Switch the automation to this Reel if it was set on a test post. Check it is ON.
- [ ] Pin my comment with the keyword.
- [ ] Comment the keyword from my second account. Reply and DM arrived? Link works?

## First hour

- [ ] Check the comments every 10 to 15 minutes.
- [ ] Reply by hand to typos, questions and anything the app missed.
- [ ] Check "View hidden comments" at the bottom of the comments, in case Hidden Words hid real ones.
- [ ] Answer anyone who replied to the DM.
- [ ] Note the comment count and DMs sent at the 1 hour mark.

## Day 2

- [ ] Reply to any comments the app missed overnight.
- [ ] Answer DM replies (the app can only send more within 24 hours of their reply, so do not wait long).
- [ ] Write in the tracker: comments, DMs sent, DM replies, new follows.
- [ ] Note 1 thing that worked and 1 thing to change.

## Day 7

- [ ] Write the day 7 numbers in the tracker: comments, DMs sent, DM replies, new follows.
- [ ] Remember: comments older than 7 days cannot get an automatic DM. Decide if you keep the automation on, or reply by hand from now on.
- [ ] Read the DM replies again. What did people ask? That is your next Reel or your next resource.
- [ ] Turn the automation off if the offer has ended (an expired code, a closed waitlist).
- [ ] Write 1 short note: what I will try differently next time.

## Learn from your own numbers

The internet is full of rules like "post at 6 PM" or "Reels must be under 30 seconds". Some may be true for some accounts. None of them are proven for you.

Your tracker is better than any rule. After 5 to 10 Reels, look at your own rows:

- Which keywords got the most comments?
- Which offers got the most DM replies?
- Which Reels brought new follows?

Change 1 thing at a time (the offer, the keyword style, the ending line) so you can tell what made the difference.
