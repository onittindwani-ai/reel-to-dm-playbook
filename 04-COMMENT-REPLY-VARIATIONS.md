# Comment Reply Variations

The public reply the app posts under each comment. It tells the person (and other viewers) that the DM is on its way.

## How to use this file

1. Start with my 6 real replies.
2. Add 10 to 20 more from the lists below that fit your Reel and your voice.
3. Swap any slot in square brackets, like `[THING]`, for your real word.
4. Load them into your app as reply variations. The app rotates them.

## My 6 real replies

These are the exact replies I use. The 4th one was written for my SHOW Reel, so swap its ending for your topic:

```
Sent! Check your DMs 🙌
```
```
Just sent it, check your inbox 📩
```
```
Done ✅ It's in your DMs now
```
```
Sent you the skill 🚀 go show, don't tell
```
```
Check your DMs 👀 it's there
```
```
Sent! Let me know how it works for you 🔥
```

## Why rotate

- **It reads like a person.** 300 copies of the same line look like a bot.
- **It is less repetitive** for people scrolling the comments.
- **It lets you add a little personality**, like "go show, don't tell" on my SHOW Reel.

Keep every reply short, friendly and true. Only say "sent" if the app really sends the DM.

## How many to load

Load **10 to 20**. Fewer than 10 starts to look repetitive on a busy Reel. More than 20 is hard to keep in your voice. Mix short ones, ones with emojis, ones without, and 2 or 3 topic specific ones.

## A quick check before you load them

- [ ] Every reply is true (only say "sent" if the app sends it).
- [ ] No reply promises a result.
- [ ] No reply asks for a second action (follow, share, save) unless your offer needs it.
- [ ] Every slot in square brackets is replaced.
- [ ] A mix of short, friendly, emoji and no emoji replies.
