# Onit's Reel to DM Playbook

My simple system for turning Instagram Reels into DMs: people comment 1 word, and they get something useful in their inbox.
This folder has the exact way I pick the keyword, write the DM, and write the comment reply variations.

## Who this is for

- Instagram creators who make Reels and want a real conversation with the people who watch them.
- Small businesses, coaches and educators who have something useful to give away (a guide, a template, a code).
- You, if you have ever said "comment below" and then had to send every DM by hand.

You do not need to code. You do not need a paid tool to start.

## Use it in 10 minutes

1. Pick the 1 thing you will give away. It must match your Reel. (Read the "offer" part of `01-THE-PLAYBOOK.md`.)
2. Pick your keyword with the 5 question test in `02-KEYWORD-PICKER.md`.
3. Copy a DM template from `03-DM-TEMPLATES.md` and fill in the slots.
4. Copy 10 to 20 comment replies from `04-COMMENT-REPLY-VARIATIONS.md`.
5. Set it up in a free app (I use Vyral) with `05-VYRAL-SETUP.md`, and test it with a second account.
6. Say the keyword at the end of your Reel, put it in the caption and the pinned comment, then follow `07-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md`.

## 2 ways to use it

### Way 1: give it to Claude

Give Claude the GitHub link to this playbook and say "set this playbook up for my next Reel". Add 1 line about your Reel, like this:

```
https://github.com/onittindwani-ai/reel-to-dm-playbook
Set this playbook up for my next Reel. My Reel is about a free app that turns voice notes into notes. I will give away my setup guide, and the link is in my Notion.
```

Change the Reel topic, the resource and the link to your own.

Claude reads the files and makes your keyword, DM, replies and checklist. You do not need to install anything by hand.

### Way 2: paste the prompt

Open `06-PROMPT-MAKE-YOUR-KIT.md`, copy the big prompt, fill in your 5 answers, and paste it into ChatGPT or Claude.
