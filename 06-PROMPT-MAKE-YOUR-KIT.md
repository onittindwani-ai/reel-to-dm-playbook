# Prompt: Make Your Reel to DM Kit

1 copy paste prompt that writes your whole kit in ChatGPT or Claude: keyword, ending line, DM, replies, pinned comment, caption and follow up.

## How to use this file

1. Copy the prompt below.
2. Fill in the 5 answers at the top (the parts in square brackets).
3. Paste it into ChatGPT or Claude.
4. Read the output. Check every fact, link and promise yourself before you use it.

## The prompt

```
You are helping me set up a comment to DM system for 1 Instagram Reel.
Follow every rule below. Write in plain, friendly, simple English (about grade 5).
Short sentences. Write every number as a numeral. Do not use em dashes or en dashes.
No hype words (insane, game changer, guaranteed, secret). Do not promise results.

MY 5 ANSWERS
1. Reel topic: [WHAT THE REEL IS ABOUT]
2. Audience: [WHO WATCHES IT]
3. The resource I give away: [WHAT IT IS, IN 1 LINE]
4. The link: [THE LINK]
5. Tone: [FOR EXAMPLE: warm and casual, or calm and expert]

KEYWORD RULES
- 1 word, 3 to 6 letters, easy to type on a phone.
- Names the thing they get or the action they want.
- Not a word people type by accident (yes, nice, wow, link, love, info, me).
- Not the product name. Not a spam word (free, win, money, promo).
- No numbers, symbols or emojis.

DM RULES (4 parts, under about 70 words, exactly 1 link)
1. A warm 1 line hello that names the thing (use the same name as the Reel).
2. The link on its own line.
3. 1 line on what it does for them.
4. The easiest way to use it, in 1 or 2 lines.
If the link is an affiliate link or a paid partnership, add 1 honest disclosure line.

WHAT TO GIVE ME (use these exact headings)

1. KEYWORD OPTIONS
A table of 5 keyword options. For each, score these 5 checks as yes or no:
1 word with 3 to 6 letters / easy to type / names the thing or action /
not typed by accident / not the product name or a spam word.
Then a total out of 5 and 1 short reason.
Then: BEST PICK: the keyword with the top score, and 1 line on why.

2. SPOKEN ENDING LINE
1 line I say at the end of the Reel, in this shape:
"If you want (what they get), (why it helps or what is inside), then just comment the word (KEYWORD)."

3. THE DM
The DM using the 4 part formula. Then the word count.

4. 20 COMMENT REPLIES
A numbered list of 20 short public replies that say the DM is sent.
Mix: 6 short, 5 friendly, 4 with emojis, 3 without emojis, 2 that mention the resource by name.
Include 2 that say "check your message requests" for people who do not follow me.

5. PINNED COMMENT
1 line I pin under the Reel that repeats the keyword and what they get.

6. CAPTION
1 line caption with the keyword, then 3 to 5 relevant hashtags.

7. FOLLOW UP MESSAGE
1 short message for when someone replies "thanks" to the DM, ending with 1 easy question.

8. CHECKS
A list of anything I must check myself before posting (links, claims, disclosure).
```

## Worked example 1: AI tool Reel
