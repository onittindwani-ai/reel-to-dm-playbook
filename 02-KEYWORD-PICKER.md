# Keyword Picker

How I pick the 1 word people comment. Use it before every Reel.

## How to use this file

1. Read my pattern and the rules (2 minutes).
2. Write 3 to 5 keyword ideas for your Reel.
3. Run each one through the 5 question test. Keep the one that scores 5 out of 5.
4. Check it against the bad keyword list.

## My pattern, from my real keywords

| Keyword | My Reel was about | What people got |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SHOW | My Show, Don't Tell AI skill | The free skill on GitHub |
| VOICE | A free voice cloning app | My setup guide and a consent checklist |
| AUTO | Vyral, a free comment to DM app | This playbook |

What these 3 have in common:

- Each is 1 short word (4 or 5 letters).
- Each names the thing (VOICE, AUTO) or the idea (SHOW, from "show, don't tell").
- None is the product name. I did not use VYRAL as the keyword.
- None is a word people type by accident in a normal comment.
- I said the same word out loud, put it on screen, in the caption and in the pinned comment.

## The rules

1. **1 word.** Not 2. Not a phrase.
2. **3 to 6 letters.** Short is easy to type with 1 thumb.
3. **Easy to type on a phone.** No odd spellings, no numbers, no symbols.
4. **Names the thing or the action.** VOICE for a voice guide. PLAN for a plan. COOK for a recipe pack.
5. **Not a word people type by accident.** Not "yes", "nice", "wow", "love", "link?", "info", "me".
6. **Not the product name.** Product names are long, tricky to spell, and fire on every comment that mentions the product.
7. **Not a common spam word.** Not "free", "win", "money", "cash", "promo".
8. **The same word everywhere.** Spoken, on screen, caption, pinned comment and the app trigger.
9. **1 keyword per Reel.** One word, one offer.
10. **Do not reuse within a week.** A fresh word for each Reel keeps it clear which offer it is.

## The 5 question test

Answer yes or no. You need 5 yeses.

- [ ] 1. Is it 1 word with 3 to 6 letters?
- [ ] 2. Could someone type it right on the first try, on a phone, without seeing it written?
- [ ] 3. Does it name the thing they get or the action they want?
- [ ] 4. Is it a word people would NOT type by accident under a Reel (not yes, nice, wow, link, love, free)?
- [ ] 5. Is it different from the product name and from my keywords used in the last 7 days?

Tip: say it out loud to a friend once. If they ask "how do you spell that?", pick another word.

## Bad keywords, and why

| Bad keyword | Why it fails |
| --- | --- |
| YES | People type it by accident when agreeing with you |
| NICE | A normal reaction comment, fires for people who did not ask |
| WOW | Same as NICE |
| LINK or LINK? | People type it anyway, and with a question mark it may not match |
| INFO | Vague, and looks like spam |
| FREE | A spam word, and vague |
| WIN | Looks like a giveaway, attracts spam |
| ME | Too short, too common |
| AUTOMATION | Too long, easy to misspell |
| VYRAL | It is the product name, and people mention it in normal comments |
| GUIDE2026 | Numbers are slow to type on a phone |
| SHOWDONTTELL | Too long, and people will add spaces or an apostrophe |
| ❤️ | Emojis are hard to match and people use them all the time |
| DM | People write "DM me" and "I'll DM you" in normal comments |
| CHATGPTPROMPTS | Too long, product name inside |
