# Vyral Setup (Free, on Your Phone)

How to set up comment to DM for free with Vyral, the app I use. Facts below come from Vyral's own page (vyral.co.in/instagram-autodm) and its app store listings, read on 19 October 2026.

## How to use this file

1. Have your keyword (`02-KEYWORD-PICKER.md`), your DM (`03-DM-TEMPLATES.md`) and 10 to 20 replies (`04-COMMENT-REPLY-VARIATIONS.md`) ready.
2. Follow the 5 steps below.
3. Do the "test it before you post" routine. Never skip it.

## What Vyral is

What Vyral says about itself, in plain words:

- It is a free AutoDM app for **iOS and Android**. On iOS it is listed as **"Vyral Creator"**.
- When someone comments your keyword, it **sends them a DM**.
- You can send **different DMs for different keywords** (they call this "Smart Keyword Triggers").
- It can **auto reply to comments**.
- It has **Follow Unlock**: the person follows you first, then gets the guide ("Follow first, then get the guide").
- You can send a **PDF** or a **waitlist** in the DM.

My own experience: I use Vyral myself. It was completely free for me, and the DMs on my past Reel were sent by it. I am not paid by Vyral and they have not endorsed me or this playbook. Vyral describes itself in its own marketing; I only repeat the features above.

## Before you start

- [ ] Your Instagram account is a **professional account** (business or creator). Meta only lets these apps work with professional accounts. You can switch in Instagram's settings, under account type and tools.
- [ ] You have your keyword, DM and replies written.
- [ ] Your resource link works in a private browser window.
- [ ] You have a second Instagram account (or a friend) for testing.

## The 5 steps (Vyral's own steps)

Exact button names may be different in your version of the app. If a button has moved, look for the closest match.

### Step 1: Get the app

Download Vyral from the App Store ("Vyral Creator") or Google Play. Check the developer name and the reviews so you get the real app.

### Step 2: Link your account in 1 tap

Connect your Instagram account. Instagram will show you what the app is asking for (reading comments, sending messages). Read it before you agree.

### Step 3: Pick a workflow

Choose the type of automation you want, for example "comment keyword sends a DM". If you want people to follow first, pick a Follow Unlock style workflow.

### Step 4: Add your keyword

- Type your keyword, for example `AUTO`.
- If the app lets you, add common variants like `auto` and `Auto`. Some apps match any capital letters and some do not, so check with a test comment.
- Paste your DM.
- Paste your 10 to 20 comment replies.
- If you share a PDF or a waitlist, attach it here.

### Step 5: Turn it on for your Reel

- Pick the Reel (or set it up for your next post if the app offers that).
- Turn it on.
- Check the screen shows the right Reel, the right keyword and the right DM.

## Test it before you post

Do this every time. It takes 5 minutes and saves you a broken launch.

1. **From your second account, follow your main account** (so you see the Inbox version).
2. Comment the keyword exactly: `AUTO`.
3. Check: did the comment reply appear? Did the DM arrive? Does the link open?
4. Comment with a typo or extra text: `auto 🔥`. Note what happens.
5. **Unfollow from the second account** and comment again (or use a third account that does not follow you). Check the DM lands in **message requests**. That is Meta's rule for non followers.
6. If you use Follow Unlock, test it from an account that does not follow you.
7. Open the link from the DM on the second phone. Check it opens without a login.
8. Delete your test comments if you want a clean comment section. (This only removes your own test comments.)

If you test on an old Reel first, remember to switch the automation to the new Reel before you post.

## Privacy

You are giving an app access to your Instagram comments and DMs. Before you connect any app:

- [ ] Read its privacy policy. Look for what it stores and for how long.
- [ ] Check you can disconnect it and delete your data.
- [ ] Only connect apps from developers you can find and trust.
- [ ] Never share your Instagram password with an app. Connecting should happen through Instagram's own login screen.
- [ ] Remove access for apps you stop using. Instagram lists connected apps in its settings.
